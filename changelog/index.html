Updates

A log of real changes. A note is added when we ship something, not on a content calendar.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

September 23, 2026

Published the reading layer around the existing tools. Practice, Learn, the three games, rankings, and profile export are still the same product. This shipment did not add ad units and did not request an advertising review.

There is now a crawlable About page naming Eric Lewis as the publisher, stating that the tools are free to use and that the site is intended to be ad-supported, and giving sales@sumnubooks.com as the contact for corrections and privacy requests. The guides index links the new pages.

  • Practice modes — Timed, Words, and Code, and when each test ends
  • Learn hub — the six-lesson order and a 15-minute day
  • Short introductions for home row, top row, bottom row, full alphabet, numbers, and punctuation
  • Games — Word Fall, Survival, and Type Racer in one guide
  • How to improve typing speed, what WPM means here, and why speed matters, each at its own address instead of only on the home page

Privacy and terms are real addresses, with the date moved to this shipment. The advertising section now matches the product: the AdSense script loads, placeholders are labeled, and live units were not switched on. robots.txt and sitemap.xml list these pages so a crawler can find them without running the lessons.

Earlier

The site already had the practice test, six lessons, three games, rankings, profile export, and in-app copies of the privacy and terms text dated May 2025. Those tools were not rewritten for this log. Older sentences that lived only on the home page are still on the home page. The guides are the stable links. If a guide and a home paragraph ever disagree about the WPM formula, trust What is WPM.

How this log will grow

The next entry will be written when a guide, a scoring description, or a legal page actually changes. A typo inside a word list may not get a paragraph. A new claim about what a score means will. We will not publish daily posts to make the site look freshly tended. If the product drifts away from a sentence in a guide, that is a bug in the writing or a bug in the tool. Both are worth an email to sales@sumnubooks.com.
