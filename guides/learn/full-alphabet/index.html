Full alphabet

Fourth lesson. All 26 letters, in ordinary words.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This is the first lesson that looks like reading. The list is common English: the, and, have, with, from, they, would, there. Every letter row is allowed. The difficulty is the mix, not a rare spelling. “From” wants a top-row R and a home-row F, and the next word may want the row you just left. The skill is returning to the bumps between words without a glance.

If the row lessons felt easy and this one stalls, those earlier passes were probably done by looking. Go back to the row that produces the miss. A repeated miss on one letter is a finger problem, not a vocabulary problem. When the line is accurate, take a Timed test on Easy and stay near 95 percent before you care about WPM.
