Top row

Second lesson. Keys emphasized: Q W E R T Y U I O P

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Top row is a reach, not a new rest. Fingers go up and come back to A S D F and J K L semicolon. The list uses words that need the upper keys: we, you, type, quiet, write, poetry, quote. A word is finished only if the finger that reached has returned. Leaving the left index parked near R is how the bumps disappear by line three.

Q is the left pinky, W the ring, E the middle, R and T the left index. Y and U are the right index, I the middle, O the ring, P the pinky. People swap T and Y. If “type” feels like one hand is doing everything, both letters went to the same finger. Slow that word until the hands alternate.

This lesson stays locked until home row is done in this browser. If the line is mostly errors, repeat home row instead of treating the unlock as skill.
