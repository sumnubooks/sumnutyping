Home row

The first lesson. Keys: A S D F and J K L ;

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Home row is the rest position. On a QWERTY board the left hand sits on A, S, D, and F, and the right hand on J, K, L, and semicolon. F and J have a bump you can find without looking. The drill stays inside those keys: aaa, jjj, then short words such as sad, flask, flags, and hall. You will not see Q or a comma here. That limit is the lesson.

Pinkies take A and semicolon, ring fingers S and L, middle fingers D and K, index fingers F and J. A thumb takes space. The usual miss is parking the index fingers on G and H and calling that home. Those keys are reaches. If “flask” makes you look down, replant on the bumps and type it slower.

The top row stays locked in this browser until you finish this lesson. A clean slow pass matters more than the WPM at the end.
