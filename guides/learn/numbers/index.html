Numbers

Fifth lesson. Digits 0 through 9, including a few longer figures.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

The list is figures: single digits, 100, 2024, 3.14, 1234. One item includes a decimal, so this is not only the number keys. The diagram gives 1 to the left pinky, 2 to the ring, 3 to the middle, 4 and 5 to the left index, 6 and 7 to the right index, 8 to the middle, 9 to the ring, and 0 to the right pinky. Reach up from home row and come back. Do not hover on the digits.

The usual cheat is stabbing the number row with an index finger while looking. If you cannot type 45 with your eyes on the screen, you are still searching. A high letter WPM does not transfer by itself. The alphabet lesson has to be finished first. The lock in this browser enforces that.
